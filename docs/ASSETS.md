# Visual assets

Wallpaper and unlock emblem generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-06 for this project. Character artwork is unofficial; Hello Kitty and related rights belong to Sanrio. The source code license does not grant character or trademark rights.

The wallpaper was converted to WebP for a small install; the unlock emblem was resized to 512 pixels wide with its alpha preserved. The generated originals are 1672×941 and 1319×1192 pixels respectively. They are not native 4K files.

`preview.png` is a programmatically rendered palette/desktop illustration. `preview-unlock.png` is an illustrative boot layout, not a capture of an installed system.

## Wallpaper prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual wallpaper for a Hello Kitty Omarchy Linux desktop theme, not a screenshot or mockup. Create a polished minimalist widescreen 16:9 wallpaper, ideally 3840x2160. Soft warm cream (#FFF7FA) background with a barely visible blush gingham grid and tiny sparse cherry-pink bows near the outer edges. Recognizable classic Hello Kitty, white face, black eyes and whiskers, small yellow oval nose, no mouth, pink bow, sitting in the lower right quadrant holding a small pink heart. Crisp flat 2D kawaii stationery illustration, delicate dark plum outlines, subtle paper texture, clean vector-like silhouette. The character occupies about one quarter of image height; leave the entire left two thirds and central upper area quiet for working windows. Pink (#C13D71), pale blush (#F8D9E5), ivory and plum (#493442). No other characters. No words, lettering, signatures, logos, frame, desktop panels, icons, UI, or watermarks. Refined Sanrio stationery aesthetic, restrained and calm.

## Unlock emblem prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: transparent boot/unlock emblem for a Hello Kitty Omarchy desktop theme. Primary request: one isolated recognizable Hello Kitty head, classic white cat face with no mouth, two black oval eyes, three whiskers on each cheek, tiny warm yellow oval nose, a cherry-pink bow on viewer's right ear. Clean flat 2D Sanrio stationery aesthetic, smooth dark plum (#493442) outlines, pink (#C13D71) bow with pale pink center. Centered square composition with generous transparent padding, a compact crisp silhouette readable at 160 pixels wide. Only the head and bow; no body, text, shadow, badges, border, watermark or extra decorations. The outside background must be truly transparent; the white face remains opaque.
